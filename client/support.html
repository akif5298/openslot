Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel or reschedule a booking?

Go to My Bookings, open the session card, then choose cancel or reschedule before the slot start time.

How can I sync OpenSlot with Google Calendar?

Calendar integration is planned. For now, use the session details page to copy date/time and link manually.

What should I do if my professor is not showing up?

Use the notes/contact link in your session details and provide appointment ID and time for support follow-up.

Can I book recurring sessions?

Recurring booking is not yet enabled in this milestone. You can book each available slot individually.
